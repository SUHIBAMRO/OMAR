1. We need one final accuracy-versus-resolution table using the final retrained checkpoints only. For each resolution, please compare FEM and NO against the same fine reference in displacement L2, H1/energy norm, reaction force and stress. For the stress comparison, I would prefer Cauchy stress rather than PK1 as the main engineering quantity. We need avoid the pointwise maximum stress as the main design QoI. At corners and kinks this can be singular and therefore mesh-dependent even for FEM. I suggest defining a fixed physical region around the stress concentration and reporting, in addition to the Cauchy-stress field error, a robust local quantity such as a volume/area-weighted average or 95/99th percentile (maybe  the average of the highest 1%) of the relevant Cauchy stress. The region must remain fixed in physical space across resolutions. A true maximum can still be reported separately.
