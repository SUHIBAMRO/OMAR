Subject: Automated verification framework: technical report and questions

Dear Professor Rabczuk,

Following your suggestion, I have built a first version of the verification agent for the automated verification framework, using the GRAFT paper as the structural model. Please find attached a technical report with the full details and all experiments.

In brief:

1. B1: all smooth quantities converge with a demonstrated rate (uncertainty at most 0.02%), and the observed orders agree with corner theory. The corner peak stress grows as predicted by the singularity and is not usable as a reference.
2. B3 (eight levels, up to 59,400 Q20 elements): the reaction moment and strain energy converge with a rate (uncertainty about 0.15%). The groove-region averages over the current sharp sphere are stable to about 1-2.4% but show no convergence rate. A Gaussian-weighted average of the same stresses is rate-certified (uncertainty 0.11-0.18%) and robust across six refinement strategies.
3. Even the current sphere averages are accurate enough to show that the surrogates fail at the groove, where their errors exceed 600%.

The report also lists what is still missing for a complete framework: a real literature agent, tests on independent benchmarks, surrogate active learning with multi-fidelity Bayesian optimisation, and an independent verification of the results.

I would be grateful for your view on the following:

1. Should the B3 groove reference be redefined as a Gaussian-weighted region average? This would change the quantity reported in the manuscript, so I have not changed the manuscript.
2. Is a margin of 3 (reference uncertainty at most one third of the surrogate error) acceptable as the acceptance criterion?
3. Should the manuscript's wording be narrowed to "region-averaged normal stresses"?
4. Which step should come next: tests on independent benchmarks, a literature agent, surrogate active learning, or an independent verification of the present results?

Best regards,
Omar
