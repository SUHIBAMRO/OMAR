Subject: Verification agent: diagnosis, independent checks, a second held-out benchmark, and a correction

Dear Professor Rabczuk,

Thank you for your detailed reply. I have worked through your points in the order of your priorities. The updated technical report is attached; Section 20 maps each of your points to what was done.

First, a correction. While building an independent 3D assembly for the mixed-formulation check, I found that the stored B3 stresses had been converted to Cauchy stress twice: the FE library already returns the Cauchy stress, and our post-processing applied the conversion again. Displacements, the reaction moment, the strain energy and the surrogate-error evaluation are not affected. The groove stresses change by a few percent, and 2 of 41 verdicts change. I corrected the code and repeated every B3 solve; all numbers in the report are the corrected ones. The manuscript's Q20 groove-reference stresses are affected in the same way, and I would like to recompute them before revising the wording. The conclusion that the surrogates' groove-stress errors are hundreds of percent does not change.

What was done:

1. Diagnosis of non-convergence. For every quantity without a demonstrated rate, the agent now assigns a cause from five classes (mathematical/physical, discretization, solver/implementation, QoI/post-processing, surrogate) using 15 hypotheses with tests on the stored data. It runs the cheap missing tests itself and names the next investigation. It separates the B1 corner singularity from the B3 finite-radius concentration, and it reproduces all 16 causes I had established by hand.
2. Independent checks. A second FE code reproduces the B1 and B2 solvers to 1e-12, and its quadratic elements fall within the agent's stated uncertainty. A mixed F-bar formulation brackets the B3 reaction moment, but the groove stresses still differ by 3-8% between the formulations, so that question remains open.
3. Unseen benchmark. On Cook's membrane, with the specification and predictions committed before any solve and the rules unchanged, the rules predicted 4 of 5 verdicts. The miss, the strain-energy order, was diagnosed by the agent as pre-asymptotic, and a finer level supports this.
4. Local refinement at the B3 groove root (the next step the agent named) is now geometrically possible, down to h/rho = 0.15. At nu = 0.45, however, every available Q20 formulation fails on these meshes: the reduced rule shows spurious modes, the full rule locks, and F-bar is too soft. The groove peak is therefore still not verified, and a Q20/P1 or IGA discretization seems necessary.
5. Human intervention. 59 interventions are counted and categorised. Developers found 36 and LLM critics 15; only 6 steps are now fully automated.
6. Model hierarchy and related agents. There is a first registry of physical and numerical models with an escalation map. I also read FermiLink, ALL-FEM and PDE-Agents; none of them verifies a numerical reference without an exact solution.
