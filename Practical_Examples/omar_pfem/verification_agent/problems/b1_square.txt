A 2D unit square of compressible neo-Hookean material with smoothly varying
Young's modulus (constant Poisson ratio) is clamped along its bottom edge and loaded
by a spatially varying normal traction on its top edge; the vertical edges
are traction-free. The reference is computed with bilinear quadrilaterals (Q4)
on uniformly refined meshes, plus meshes of the same size graded toward the
bottom corners. Question: which displacement, energy and stress quantities
can serve as trustworthy reference values for validating surrogate models?
