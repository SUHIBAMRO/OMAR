Cook's membrane: a 2D tapered panel (vertices (0,0), (48,44), (48,60), (0,44))
of compressible neo-Hookean material in plane strain, clamped along its left
edge and loaded by a uniform shear traction along its right edge; the top and
bottom edges are traction-free. The clamped edge meets the free edges at two
corners. The reference is computed with bilinear quadrilaterals (Q4) on
uniformly refined mapped meshes, plus meshes of the same size graded toward
the clamped edge and its corners.
Question: which displacement, energy and stress quantities can serve as
trustworthy reference values for validating surrogate models?
