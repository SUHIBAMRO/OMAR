A 2D square panel with a straight edge crack running from the middle of its left edge to
the centre (the crack faces are traction-free), of linear elastic material, plane strain,
is clamped along its bottom edge and pulled by a uniform vertical traction on its top edge.
The reference is computed with biquadratic quadrilaterals (Q9) on uniformly refined meshes
with separate nodes on the two crack faces, plus meshes of the same size graded toward the
crack tip.
Question: which displacement, energy and stress quantities can serve as trustworthy
reference values for validating surrogate models?
