A 2D quarter of a thick ring (inner radius 1, outer radius 2) of compressible
neo-Hookean material, plane strain, with Young's modulus varying smoothly
around the circumference (constant Poisson ratio), is loaded by an internal
pressure that also varies smoothly around the inner arc. Symmetry conditions
hold on both straight edges; the outer arc is traction-free. The reference is
computed with bilinear quadrilaterals (Q4) on uniformly refined polar meshes,
plus meshes of the same size graded radially toward the loaded inner arc.
Question: which displacement, energy and stress quantities can serve as
trustworthy reference values for validating surrogate models?
