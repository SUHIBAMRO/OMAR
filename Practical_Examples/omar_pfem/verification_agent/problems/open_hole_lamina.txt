Open-hole composite lamina: an elliptical plate (semi-axes 50 mm and 10 mm, thickness
1 mm) with a central circular hole of radius 3 mm, made of a unidirectional carbon/epoxy
lamina (AS4/3501-6) with fibres at 15 degrees to the long axis, in plane stress. The outer
boundary carries the tractions of a homogeneous uniaxial stress along the long axis; the
hole is traction-free; three displacement components at the hole remove the rigid motions.
Linear orthotropic material. The reference is computed with quadratic 9-node
quadrilaterals on structured meshes around the hole, uniformly refined, plus meshes of the
same size with different grading toward the hole.
Question: which compliance, displacement and strain quantities can serve as trustworthy
reference values, and how large is their discretization uncertainty compared with a
constitutive uncertainty range of a few percent?
