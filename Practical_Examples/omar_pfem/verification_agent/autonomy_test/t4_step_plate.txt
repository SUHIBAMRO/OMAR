Example: stepped plate in tension. A thin plate occupies the union of the rectangles [0, 30] x [0, 10] mm and [20, 50] x [10, 20] mm (plane stress). The material is linear elastic with Young's modulus E = 210000 MPa and Poisson's ratio nu = 0.3. The left edge x = 0 is clamped. A uniform traction of 50 MPa in the x-direction acts on the right edge x = 50 mm (10 <= y <= 20 mm). All other edges are traction-free. The quantities of interest are the strain energy, the horizontal displacement of the point (50, 20) mm and the vertical displacement of the point (50, 10) mm.
