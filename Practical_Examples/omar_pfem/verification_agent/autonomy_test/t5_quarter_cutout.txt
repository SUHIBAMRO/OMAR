Example: plate with a square cut-out. A square plate of side 80 mm has a central square hole of side 40 mm whose edges are parallel to the plate edges. The plate is loaded in uniaxial tension by a uniform traction of 100 MPa in the x-direction on the edges x = -40 mm and x = 40 mm (pulling outwards); the edges y = -40 mm and y = 40 mm and the hole are traction-free. Plane strain is assumed, with Young's modulus E = 70000 MPa and Poisson's ratio nu = 0.33. Because of the double symmetry only the quarter x >= 0, y >= 0 is modelled, with symmetry conditions on the lines x = 0 and y = 0. The quantities of interest are the strain energy of the quarter model, the horizontal displacement at (40, 0) mm and the vertical displacement at (40, 40) mm.
