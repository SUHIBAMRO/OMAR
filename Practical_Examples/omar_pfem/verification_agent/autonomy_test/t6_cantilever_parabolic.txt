Example: Timoshenko cantilever. A cantilever beam of length L = 48 and height D = 12 (unit thickness, plane stress) is made of a material with E = 30000000 and nu = 0.3. The beam occupies [0, 48] x [-6, 6]; the edge x = 0 is clamped and the free end x = 48 carries a total shear force P = 1000 distributed parabolically over the height, t_y(y) = -(3P / (2D)) (1 - 4 y^2 / D^2). The quantity of interest is the vertical tip displacement at (48, 0).
