## Reading

- Framework bugs were found almost entirely by the developer while running the framework on real data; one by the LLM advisor and one batch by the claims-check script. The framework does not yet test itself for such bugs.
- Most design changes came from LLM critic objections, but every one was judged and implemented by the developer; the critics never approved a plan.
- The manual diagnostic investigations of the B1/B3 non-convergence are the part that is now largely automated (diagnose.py, 16/16 agreement with the manual causes). The expensive re-solves (full integration, surface study, new mesh levels) are not yet actions the agent can start.
- Wrong inputs (problem/adapter descriptions) were the main source of wrong LLM behaviour (I1, I3). The framework cannot detect a wrong fact that is internally consistent (I3 was found by the developer).
- Decisions on acceptance margins, QoI definitions, manuscript wording and billed runs stay with people by design.

Limits of this count: it is reconstructed from the log after the fact; small fixes not recorded in the log are missing, so the numbers are lower bounds. Effort (hours) per intervention was not recorded.
